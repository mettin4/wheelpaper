ROUNDS AND BLOCKS

Measurement rounds, 10 minutes

Squeek blocks, filled by work

Ethereum blocks, ~12 seconds

Work is counted in 10-minute rounds. A round can hold at most 10,000 work units. Rounds fill Squeek blocks. Ethereum just records the result. Three clocks, none of them waiting for the others.

THE SQUEEKS

Minted SQK is shared by the 9,999 Squeek NFTs, by power. Two Squeeks of the same level merge into one of the next. The donor burns.
